PushToLog Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026

PushToLog is an Android app that turns what you say into short text logs saved with the time you tapped. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: PushToLog does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.

Information the app does not collect

PushToLog has no user accounts, no advertising, and no analytics. The app does not request internet access, so it cannot send any information off your device itself.

Microphone and speech

PushToLog uses the microphone only after you tap its widget, its Quick Settings button, or the record button in the app. Listening stops after a pause in speech, a second tap, or two minutes at most.

While you speak, the audio is passed to the speech recognition service installed on your phone (for example, Speech Services by Google), which returns the words as text. PushToLog does not save or keep any audio recordings. The speech recognition service is a separate app with its own privacy policy, and it may process audio online. For fully offline recognition, download your language in your phone's voice or speech settings.

Information stored on your device

The following is saved only on your phone so the app can work:

  • Your logs: one plain text file per day, each line holding a time and the text of that log. These files are saved in the folder you choose.
  • If that folder cannot be reached, logs are kept briefly in the app's private storage and moved to your folder once it is available again.
  • App settings, such as your chosen folder, speech language, ring colour and widget options, and a copy of your latest log so the widget can show it.

Uninstalling the app deletes its settings and private storage. Log files in the folder you chose are your own files and stay there until you delete them.

Device backup

If Android backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include PushToLog's settings in your personal device backup. That backup is managed by Android and your Google account, not by the developer, and the developer has no access to it. Log files in your chosen folder are backed up only if you back up that folder yourself.

Permissions

PushToLog asks only for permissions it needs to record a log: the microphone, a foreground service that keeps the microphone on while you are speaking (often while another app is on screen), and vibration to confirm each log. Access to your log folder is granted only to the folder you pick. None of these are used to collect information.

Children

PushToLog is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.
