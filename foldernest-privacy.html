FolderNest Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026

FolderNest is a home-screen widget for Android that holds a folder of app shortcuts. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: FolderNest does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.

Information the app does not collect

FolderNest has no user accounts, no advertising, and no analytics. The app does not request internet access, so it cannot send any information off your device.

Apps installed on your device

To let you choose which apps go in a folder, FolderNest reads the list of apps on your phone that have a home-screen icon, along with their names and icons. This list is used only to show the app picker and to draw the icons in your widgets. It is never stored as a list and never leaves your device.

When you tap an icon in a widget, FolderNest simply opens that app. It does not see or record what you do in the app.

Information stored on your device

The following is saved only on your phone so your widgets can work:

  • For each folder widget: the apps you chose and their order, the layout, background colour and opacity, icon shape, and whether app names are shown

This data stays on your device. Removing a widget deletes its settings, and uninstalling the app deletes all of them.

Device backup

If Android backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include FolderNest's settings in your personal device backup. That backup is managed by Android and your Google account, not by the developer, and the developer has no access to it.

Permissions

FolderNest does not request any special permissions. Android tells the app when an app is uninstalled, so that its icon can be removed from your folders. This is not used to collect information.

Children

FolderNest is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
