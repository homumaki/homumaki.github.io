PushToDo — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026

To work, PushToDo saves the following on your device only, inside the app's private storage:

  • Your tasks, folders, folder colours, reminder times and repeat days
  • Widget settings (which folder each widget shows, its colour and background)
  • App settings (list order, speech language, first day of the week)

This information is not sent to the developer or to anyone else. PushToDo does not request internet access, so it cannot send data over the network.

Microphone and speech

When you tap the microphone button, PushToDo asks your phone's speech recognition service (on most phones this is provided by Google) to turn what you say into text. The microphone is used only while the voice screen is open.

  • PushToDo does not record, save or upload audio.
  • Only the resulting text is saved, as your task, on your device.
  • The speech recognition service is part of your phone, not of PushToDo, and is covered by its provider's own privacy policy (for Google: policies.google.com/privacy). If you download the speech language for offline use in your phone's settings, recognition can happen on the device.

Permissions

  • Microphone — to add tasks by voice, only when you tap the mic.
  • Notifications — to show your reminders.
  • Alarms & reminders — so reminders arrive at the exact time you set.
  • Run at startup — to set your reminders again after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration — a short buzz when you tick a task on a widget.

You can turn any of these off in your phone's settings. Features that need them will stop working, but the rest of the app keeps working.

Backups

If you have turned on Android's own backup (for example, backup to your Google account), Android may include PushToDo's data in that backup so it can be restored on a new phone. This is handled by Android and your backup provider; the developer cannot see it. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

Sharing, ads and tracking

PushToDo does not share, sell or transfer your information. It contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting services and no third-party tracking.

Deleting your data

You can delete tasks and folders inside the app at any time. To delete everything, clear the app's storage in your phone's settings or uninstall the app.

Children

PushToDo is a general productivity app and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy
