Privacy Policy

For Sandglass: Medication Timer

Last updated: 28 August 2026

Overview

Sandglass is designed to work without an account and to keep your information on your device. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal information.

Information stored on your device

Information you enter in the app, such as medication names and details, timer settings, reminders, and display preferences, is stored locally on your device. This information is not sent to the developer or to any external server.

Permissions

Sandglass may request permission to send notifications. This permission is used only to alert you when a medication timer ends or at reminder times you set.

Advertising, analytics, and third parties

Sandglass does not contain advertising or analytics and does not use third-party services to collect or process your information.

Data deletion

You can delete individual entries within the app. Uninstalling Sandglass removes the app and its locally stored data from your device, subject to your device's backup settings.

Changes to this policy

If the app's privacy practices change, this policy will be updated and the “Last updated” date above will be revised.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact homumaki@gmail.com.
