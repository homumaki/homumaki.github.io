Privacy Policy for MedTracker

Last updated: 21 August 2026

MedTracker stores all data locally on your device. The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

Data We Collect

None. Medication names, dosages, and dose history you enter are saved only on your device and are never sent anywhere.

Permissions

The app requests notification permission solely to alert you when a dose duration ends or at reminder times you set.

Third Parties

No advertising, no analytics, no third-party services.
