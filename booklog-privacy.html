Privacy Policy — Booklog

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Booklog is a personal library tool that records the ISBN barcodes of books you already own, so you can check whether you have a book before buying it again. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: everything stays on your device.

Information the app collects

None. Booklog does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Information stored on your device

When you save a book, the app stores the following in a private database on your device:

  • the barcode number (ISBN)
  • an optional short label you type yourself
  • the date and time you saved it
  • how it was added (camera, typed, or from an image)

This data is never sent anywhere. It is removed when you uninstall the app.

Camera

The app requests camera permission so it can read barcodes. The camera image is processed on your device in real time and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted. Barcode recognition runs entirely offline using an on-device library; no image or barcode is sent to any server.

Photos

You may read a barcode from a photo instead of using the camera. Booklog uses the Android system photo picker, so it receives only the single image you choose and does not request access to your photo library. The image is read once to find a barcode and is not copied or stored by the app.

Internet access

Booklog does not request the INTERNET permission and cannot send or receive data over a network.

Export and import

You can export your saved books to a CSV file and share it using Android's standard share sheet — for example to save it in your own cloud storage or send it to yourself. This happens only when you choose to do it, and the file goes only where you send it. Booklog has no access to the file after that. Importing reads a CSV file you select and adds its contents to your device database.

Children

Booklog is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour changes in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated and the date above changed.
