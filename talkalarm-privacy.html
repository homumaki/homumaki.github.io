TalkAlarm Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 September 2026

TalkAlarm is an alarm clock app for Android that speaks the current time. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: TalkAlarm does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.

Information the app does not collect

TalkAlarm has no user accounts, no advertising, and no analytics. The app does not request internet access, so it cannot send any information off your device.

Information stored on your device

The following is saved only on your phone so the app can work:

  • Your alarms: times, repeat settings, language, message text, sound mode, snooze and vibration settings
  • App settings, such as nap settings and your chosen accent colour
  • If you choose Music mode, a reference to the sound file you picked (the file itself is not copied)

This data stays on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Device backup

If Android backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include TalkAlarm's settings in your personal device backup. That backup is managed by Android and your Google account, not by the developer, and the developer has no access to it.

Text-to-speech

To speak the time, TalkAlarm passes the text to be spoken to the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone (for example, Speech Services by Google). That engine is a separate app with its own privacy policy. For fully offline speech, download the voice for your language in your phone's text-to-speech settings.

Permissions

TalkAlarm asks only for permissions needed to ring alarms: notifications, exact alarms, full-screen alarm display, keeping the alarm running until you snooze or dismiss it, restoring alarms after a restart, keeping the phone awake while ringing, and vibration. None of these are used to collect information.

Children

TalkAlarm is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
